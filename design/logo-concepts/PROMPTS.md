# Conceitos de logo da mila.ai

Gerados com a ferramenta de imagem integrada. São estudos visuais em PNG, ainda não arquivos vetoriais finais.

## 01 — Wordmark

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: brand identity concept for mila.ai, Brazilian WhatsApp-first business assistant for semi-jewelry shop owners.
Primary request: original premium wordmark logo concept, exact text "mila.ai" in lowercase. Make typography itself the identity: soft geometric sans lettering with carefully custom-shaped "m", generous spacing, subtly distinctive circular dot. Quiet luxury and intelligence without looking like a jewelry boutique.
Style/medium: flat vector-like design, single plum ink color.
Composition/framing: one centered horizontal logo, ample whitespace, plain warm ivory background.
Text (verbatim): "mila.ai" exactly once, lowercase, no other letters.
Constraints: highly legible at small website header size, balanced negative space, original design. No gradients, shine, gold, gemstone, speech bubble, mockup, watermark.

## 02 — Monograma

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: brand identity concept for mila.ai, Brazilian WhatsApp-first business assistant for semi-jewelry shop owners.
Primary request: original monogram logo with a distinctive lowercase "m" formed from two fluid but precise continuous arches; a single tiny round accent integrated into the upper right evokes a helpful presence. Include exact wordmark "mila.ai" beside mark, clean geometric lowercase sans.
Style/medium: flat vector-like mark and wordmark, deep plum on warm ivory.
Composition/framing: centered horizontal lockup with symbol left, wordmark right, generous space. Crisp edges.
Text (verbatim): "mila.ai" exactly once.
Constraints: symbol must remain recognizable as m at small WhatsApp avatar size; confident and contemporary, not ornate. No sparkle, diamonds, crown, chatbot face, gradient, 3D, mockup, watermark.

## 03 — Conversa e análise

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: brand identity concept for mila.ai, Brazilian WhatsApp-first business assistant for semi-jewelry shop owners.
Primary request: original minimal brand mark uniting conversation and clear business analysis: an elegant open rounded chat-shaped contour whose negative space creates a tiny upward precision notch or price tick, paired with exact lowercase wordmark "mila.ai". The mark should feel like a subtle signature, not an app icon cliché.
Style/medium: flat vector-like design, deep plum with one muted rose accent maximum, on plain warm ivory.
Composition/framing: centered horizontal lockup, symbol left and wordmark right, generous padding.
Text (verbatim): "mila.ai" exactly once.
Constraints: simple strong silhouette, legible at favicon size, original. No literal jewelry, no robot, no WhatsApp logo, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no watermark.
